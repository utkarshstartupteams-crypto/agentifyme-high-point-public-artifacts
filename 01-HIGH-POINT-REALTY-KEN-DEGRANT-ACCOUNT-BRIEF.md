# **Account Snapshot**

**High Point Realty & Auction** is a Kentucky real-estate and auction company serving Central and Eastern Kentucky. Its current public website presents two main service lines: **real-estate sales** for buyers and sellers, and **onsite/online auctions** . The website identifies **Ken DeGrant as Broker/Owner** , with John Montgomery and Bradley Conner listed as auctioneers.

The company currently serves counties including Madison, Estill, Rockcastle, Jackson, Lee, Breathitt, Powell and Owsley. Its website states that live MLS listings are still being connected and currently directs visitors to contact the team directly for available properties while linking separately to current auctions.

# **Ken DeGrant**

Ken is publicly listed as **Broker/Owner** of High Point Realty & Auction. The company describes him as having more than a decade of experience serving Kentucky buyers and sellers across residential, commercial and auction real estate.

Recent public listings also show Ken as the listing broker for current properties under the High Point Realty & Auction name. For example, Realtor.com currently lists him as the broker for a Booneville property distributed through ImagineMLS.

# **Services**

We can verify at least three customer-facing areas:

- **Residential and commercial real-estate sales**
    
- **Live / online real-estate auctions**
    
- **Other auction activity beyond real estate**
    

High Point's website explicitly advertises residential and commercial sales plus onsite and online auctions.

HiBid listings show High Point running real-estate auctions, including current and recent properties in Irvine and Ravenna, Kentucky.

Public HiBid records also show High Point conducting non-real-estate auctions, such as estatesale collectibles and other items, which means its auction operations may be broader than property sales alone.

That gives us an important discovery question later:

## **Does Ken want Speed-to-Lead to support only property inquiries, or could auction inquiries also matter?**

We should not assume the answer.

That gives us a plausible lead-source map:

## **Website → MLS/Realtor.com → HiBid → phone/email → possibly social/other channels**

But only the first four are currently evidenced.

# **Observable Workflow Clues**

We can observe that:

- the company website currently does not yet present live MLS listings directly and instead asks visitors to contact the team, while sending auction traffic separately to auction channels;
    
- active HiBid pages sometimes direct preview questions directly to Ken by phone;
    
- High Point has multiple named team members, but public sources do not tell us who owns which inbound lead types;
    
- current listings are syndicated through MLS/Realtor.com as well as being associated with auction workflows.
    

That creates a reasonable hypothesis:

High Point may have inquiries arriving through more than one platform and communication method, making routing, ownership and response consistency worth validating.

# **Speed-to-Lead Hypotheses**

- Multiple inquiry channels may make lead ownership harder to track consistently.
    
- Auction and normal property inquiries may require different qualification and routing rules.
    
- Direct-to-Ken inquiries could create a bottleneck when he is unavailable.
    
- High Point may value a system that responds quickly while preserving broker control over sensitive or auction-specific questions.
