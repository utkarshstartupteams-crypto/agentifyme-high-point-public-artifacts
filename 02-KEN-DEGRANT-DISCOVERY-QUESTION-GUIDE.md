# **Purpose**

Understand how High Point Realty & Auction currently receives, responds to, qualifies, routes, and follows up on inbound leads, and identify where the Speed-to-Lead prototype fits or fails.

# **Conversational Goal**

Learn Ken's real workflow before making assumptions about the product.

# **Must-Ask Questions**

## **1. Lead Sources**

**Question:** Where do most of your new buyer or property inquiries come from today? 
**Why It Matters:** Helps identify the main inbound channels AgentifyMe may need to support.

## **2. Lead Volume**

**Question:** Roughly how many inquiries do you receive in a typical week or month? 
**Why It Matters:** Helps determine whether response coverage is a meaningful operational issue.

## **3. First Response Ownership**

**Question:** When a new inquiry comes in, who normally responds first? 
**Optional Follow-Up:** Is that usually you, another agent, or whoever is available?
**Why It Matters:** Helps understand lead ownership and routing.

## **4. Response Time**

**Question:** How quickly are you usually able to respond to a new inquiry?
**Optional Follow-Up:** Are there situations where that takes longer than you’d like?
**Why It Matters:** Tests whether delayed response is a real pain point.

## **5. After-Hours Handling**

**Question:** What normally happens when someone reaches out in the evening, overnight, or while everyone is busy?

**Why It Matters:** Tests whether coverage gaps exist outside normal availability.

## **6. Missed or Delayed Lead Example**

**Question:** Can you remember a recent situation where an inquiry sat too long, went to the wrong person, or became difficult to follow up?

**Why It Matters:** Gives direct customer evidence instead of hypothetical agreement.

## **7. Qualification**

**Question:** Before you or another broker personally follows up, what information do you ideally want to know about the prospect?

**Why It Matters:** Helps identify what AgentifyMe should capture before human handoff.

## **8. Buyer Vs Auction Workflow**

**Question:** Are regular real-estate inquiries and auction inquiries handled differently? 
**Optional Follow-Up:** If they are different, what changes in the process?

**Why It Matters:** Helps determine whether one workflow can support both.

## **9. Existing Systems**

**Question:** Do you currently use a CRM, lead-routing system, automated texting tool, or another system to manage inquiries?

**Optional Follow-Up:** What works well today, and what still requires manual effort?

**Why It Matters:** Prevents us from solving a problem that is already sufficiently handled.

## **10. AI and Automation Boundaries**

**Question:** What kinds of questions would you be comfortable having an automated

system respond to before a human takes over?

**Follow-Up:** What should always go directly to you or another broker?

**Why It Matters:** Helps define guardrails and handoff rules.


**11. Prototype Relevance:** 

**Question:** How close is this prototype to how leads actually move through your business today?

**Why It Matters:** Helps identify where the current prototype matches or misses Ken's workflow.

## **12. Pilot Signal**

**Question:** If we adjusted this around your workflow, what would you need to see before you would consider testing something like this?

**Why It Matters:** Helps define what evidence would justify a second meeting or pilot.

# **Optional Questions**

## **Lead Quality**

Which lead sources tend to produce your most serious or valuable opportunities?

## **Communication Preference**

Do prospects usually respond better to phone calls, texts or email?

## **Follow-Up Cadence**

If someone does not respond initially, how often does your team normally follow up?

## **Routing Rules**

Are leads assigned based on geography, property type, availability, expertise or something else?

## **Seller Leads**

Is the first-response process different for seller inquiries compared with buyers?

## **Auction Inquiries**

What are the most common questions people ask before an auction?

## **Showing Requests**

How are showing requests currently scheduled?

## **Integrations**

If a system like this were useful, what would it need to connect with for you to actually use it?

## **Trust / Accuracy**

What would make you nervous about letting AI communicate with a prospect?

## **Lead Visibility**

Do you currently have an easy way to see which leads are still waiting for Follow-Up?

# **Minimum Question Set**

Use this if the conversation is short.

1. Where do most inquiries come from?
    
2. Who normally responds first?
    
3. How quickly are you usually able to respond?
    
4. What happens when nobody is available?
    
5. Can you give an example of a recent delayed or difficult lead?
    
6. What information do you need before useful human follow-up?
    
7. What would make this prototype useful or unrealistic for your business?

- typical response time;
    
- after-hours handling;
