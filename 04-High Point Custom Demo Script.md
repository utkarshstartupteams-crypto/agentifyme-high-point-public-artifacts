## Purpose

Provide a simple 3–5 minute walkthrough of the AgentifyMe Speed-to-Lead prototype for Ken DeGrant / High Point Realty & Auction.

The demo is intended to generate customer feedback, not simply present features.

The main questions we want to answer are:

- Does this resemble High Point's actual lead workflow?
- Is AgentifyMe collecting the right information?
- Is the broker handoff happening at the right time?
- Does the product feel simple enough to use?
- Where does the prototype differ from how High Point actually works?

---

## Demo Principle

Keep the walkthrough simple.

Show only:

1. the new lead;
2. what AgentifyMe did;
3. what the broker needs to do next.

Avoid explaining every feature or control in the interface.

---

## Demo Scenario

**Synthetic Buyer:** Sarah Miller  
**Lead Type:** Residential buyer  
**Location:** Richmond, Kentucky  
**Budget:** Up to $350,000  
**Property Preference:** 3 bedrooms, garage preferred  
**Showing Request:** Saturday around 11:00 AM  
**Broker:** Ken DeGrant

All demo information is fictional and is used only to demonstrate the proposed workflow.

---

# Demo Flow

## 1. Set the Context

**Time:** 0:00–0:30

### What to Say

> Let's imagine someone sees one of High Point's properties online and sends an inquiry while you or the appropriate agent is busy with another client.

> Instead of waiting for someone to notice the inquiry, AgentifyMe begins handling the first part of the conversation.

### Goal

Give Ken enough context to understand the situation without giving a long product introduction.

---

## 2. Show the New Lead

**Time:** 0:30–1:00

### Screen

**Simplified Leads Dashboard**

### What to Show

Point out only:

- Sarah Miller;
- lead source;
- status;
- response time;
- assigned broker.

### What to Say

> Sarah has just submitted an inquiry.

> The main idea here is that you can quickly see who the lead is, whether someone has responded, and who owns the next action.

### Do Not Explain

Avoid walking through every menu, metric, filter, or dashboard feature.

The purpose of this screen is simply:

> **A new lead came in, and I can immediately see what is happening with it.**

---

## 3. Open the Conversation

**Time:** 1:00–2:15

### Screen

**Lead Conversation / Buyer Summary**

### Buyer Inquiry

> Hi, I saw the three-bedroom property near Richmond. I'm looking for something under $350,000, preferably with a garage. Could I see it this Saturday?

### AgentifyMe Response

> Thanks for reaching out. I can help gather a few details for High Point Realty. Do you have a preferred time on Saturday, and are you currently working with another real-estate agent?

### Buyer Follow-Up

> Around 11 AM would work well. I'm not currently working with another agent.

### What to Show

Focus on:

- conversation;
- fast AI response;
- extracted buyer information;
- short buyer summary;
- `Take Over` or equivalent broker action.

### What to Say

> AgentifyMe isn't trying to replace the broker here.

> It's handling the first response and collecting useful context so that when you step in, you don't have to start the conversation from zero.

Point to the buyer summary.

> Here we already know Sarah's location, budget, basic property preferences, showing request, and that she's not currently represented.

Then point to the takeover control.

> If this is enough information, the broker can step into the conversation from here.

---

## 4. Ask the First Discovery Question

**Time:** 2:15–2:35

Pause the demo.

### Ask Ken

> Is this roughly the kind of information you would want before you personally followed up with a buyer?

### Listen For

- information that is missing;
- information that is unnecessary;
- differences in qualification;
- differences between High Point and our assumed workflow.

Do not defend the prototype if Ken says something is wrong.

---

# 5. Show the Human Handoff Example

**Time:** 2:35–3:30

### Screen

**Out-of-Scope / Human Handoff**

This should be a very short secondary example.

### Example Buyer Question

> Can you guarantee that the seller would accept a $100,000 cash offer?

### What to Show

Show that AgentifyMe:

1. recognizes the question requires human judgment;
2. avoids making the guarantee;
3. pauses or stops automated handling;
4. alerts the broker;
5. preserves the conversation context.

### What to Say

> The other side of automation is knowing when not to automate.

> If the buyer asks something that AgentifyMe shouldn't answer, it doesn't guess or make a commitment on behalf of the brokerage.

> It flags the conversation and hands it to a human.

Point to the handoff status.

> The broker can then take over with the context already preserved.

---

## 6. Ask About Automation Boundaries

**Time:** 3:30–3:50

### Ask Ken

> Are there other kinds of questions where you would always want the system to stop and bring in a human?

### Listen For

Examples may include:

- pricing;
- contractual commitments;
- auction conditions;
- legal questions;
- financing;
- negotiation;
- property-specific claims.

These answers should become product requirements or guardrail feedback.

---

# 7. Connect It Back to High Point

**Time:** 3:50–4:30

### Ask

> How close is this to what actually happens when someone contacts High Point today?

Then:

> What would need to change for this to fit your workflow?

Possible follow-up:

> Would the process be different for an auction inquiry?

### Goal

Move from showing the prototype to learning about High Point's real workflow.

---

# 8. Close the Demo

**Time:** 4:30–5:00

### What to Say

> This isn't meant to assume that this is exactly how High Point should operate.

> What we're trying to learn is where something like this could actually be useful, and where we've made the wrong assumptions.

### Final Question

> If we changed one thing in this prototype to make it more useful for High Point, what would you change first?

# Demo Success Criteria

The demo is successful if, by the end, we understand more about High Point's workflow than we did before the meeting.

Success does not require Ken to say that he likes AgentifyMe.

Useful outcomes include:

- confirming an assumption;
- disproving an assumption;
- identifying a missing requirement;
- identifying an unnecessary feature;
- defining an automation boundary;
- discovering an integration requirement;
- identifying a better demo scenario;
- identifying a reason High Point would or would not test the product.

---

# Prototype Feedback Principle

Based on current internal feedback, the demo interface should favor simplicity over feature density.

For each screen ask:

> Does the broker need this information or action to understand what is happening right now?

If not, consider hiding it from the primary demo view.
