# AgentifyMe for High Point Realty & Auction

## Speed-to-Lead Concept

### The Idea

When a new buyer inquiry comes in, the first few minutes matter.

AgentifyMe is being designed to help a brokerage respond quickly, collect useful buyer information, and make sure the right human knows what needs attention next.

The goal is not to replace the broker.

The goal is to make the first part of the lead-handling process faster, clearer, and easier to manage.

---

## What the Workflow Could Look Like

### 1. A New Inquiry Arrives

A buyer contacts High Point through an online property inquiry or another connected lead source.

### 2. AgentifyMe Responds

The buyer receives an initial response without waiting for a broker to become available.

### 3. Useful Information Is Collected

AgentifyMe can gather information such as:

- property or location of interest;
- budget;
- buyer intent;
- showing preference;
- basic qualification details.

### 4. The Broker Gets the Context

The conversation is summarized so the broker does not have to start from the beginning.

### 5. A Human Takes Over When Needed

If the conversation requires broker judgment, AgentifyMe stops and hands the conversation to the appropriate person.

---

## Potential Value for High Point

### Faster First Response

New inquiries can receive an initial response while the team is busy with clients, showings, auctions, or other work.

### Better Context Before Follow-Up

The broker can see what the buyer wants before entering the conversation.

### Clearer Lead Ownership

The system can help make it visible who is responsible for the next action.

### Human Control

The broker remains in control of sensitive, unusual, or high-value conversations.

---

## What AgentifyMe Should Not Do

AgentifyMe should not guess or make commitments on behalf of the brokerage when human judgment is required.

Examples may include:

- legal questions;
- financial advice;
- contractual commitments;
- negotiation decisions;
- unverified property information;
- other questions High Point wants handled personally.

In these cases, the system should hand the conversation to a human.

---

## What We Want to Learn From High Point

This concept is not intended to assume how High Point currently operates.

We want to understand:

- where inquiries come from today;
- who normally responds first;
- what information is useful before follow-up;
- what happens after hours or when the team is busy;
- whether auction inquiries require a different workflow;
- where automation would be useful;
- where automation should stop.

---

## The Goal

A simple workflow where:

**New Inquiry → Quick Response → Useful Context → Clear Handoff → Human Follow-Up**

The product should reduce friction without making the broker's job more complicated.
